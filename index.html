The Problem

Finding Medicine Shouldn’t Be This Difficult

🔍

Uncertain Availability

Medicine availability is uncertain — you may travel to a pharmacy only to find the medicine out of stock.

💰

Varying Prices

Prices can vary significantly between pharmacies for the same medicine, making it hard to find the best deal.

🚶

Unnecessary Travel

Customers may travel long distances to find medicines that are actually available closer to home.

🚨

Emergency Access

Finding an open pharmacy during emergencies, especially late at night, can be extremely difficult.

💊

Forgotten Doses

Forgetting scheduled medicines is common — and family members far away often have no way to know.

🏥

Hospital Discovery

Finding a nearby hospital with directions and a call option during an urgent situation is harder than it should be.

🤖

No Quick Guidance

There is rarely a quick, easy-to-understand source for general medicine information and platform help.

CareLink

CareLink Keeps Families Connected

Even when family members are far away, CareLink helps selected caregivers stay informed when configured medicine reminders are repeatedly missed.

⏰Medicine reminder
→
✅Taken → Normal
→
⚠️Skipped → Missed
→
💛Repeated misses → Caregiver notified

CareLink notifies selected caregivers when the configured missed-dose condition is reached. It is a family-connection feature, not an emergency medical alert.
